# HealthSense AI — Limitations & Uncertainty Disclosure (V2)
=============================================================

1. **Not a Clinical Device**: HealthSense AI is an educational decision-support tool, not a medical device. It does not possess clinical certifications (e.g. FDA 510(k), CE mark).
2. **Kidney Disease (Renal Diet)**: Standard food nutrition facts labels do NOT report potassium or bioavailable phosphorus additives. Therefore, packaged food digital data is legally and clinically INSUFFICIENT to establish renal suitability. Consultation with a nephrologist or specialized renal dietitian is mandatory.
3. **Allergen Risks**: Traces and cross-contact risks cannot be verified solely from digital databases. Users with severe IgE-mediated allergies must physically inspect package labeling and contact manufacturers.
4. **Total Sugar vs. Free/Added Sugar**: Many jurisdictions only require total sugars to be declared. HealthSense AI V2 does not guess or invent added sugar values when they are omitted.
5. **Serving Size Discrepancies**: Manufacturer serving sizes vary significantly across brands. The app standardizes per 100g and notes per-serving amounts where reported.
6. **Crowdsourced Data Variance**: Open Food Facts relies on user contributions. While barcode data is verified where possible, nutritional information may contain OCR errors or outdated formulations.
