# HealthSense AI — Evidence Sources & Standards Registry (V2)
==============================================================

Every rule, threshold, and finding in HealthSense AI V2 is traceable to an official public health authority.

| Evidence ID | Authority | Source Document | Standard Type | Application Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EVID_WHO_SUGARS_2015` | **WHO** | Guideline: Sugars intake for adults and children (2015) | Population Intake | Free sugars < 10% total energy (strong), < 5% (conditional) |
| `EVID_WHO_SODIUM_2023` | **WHO** | WHO Global report on sodium intake reduction (2023) | Population Intake | Sodium < 2,000 mg/day (< 5g salt/day) for adults |
| `EVID_WHO_SODIUM_CHILDREN` | **WHO** | Sodium intake for adults and children (2012) | Pediatric Intake | Downward scaled (< 1,500mg/day for school-age) |
| `EVID_WHO_SFA_2023` | **WHO** | SFA and TFA intake for adults and children (2023) | Population Intake | Saturated fat < 10% daily energy |
| `EVID_WHO_TFA_2023` | **WHO** | SFA and TFA intake for adults and children (2023) | Regulatory Limit | Trans fatty acids < 1% energy; iTFA < 2% of total fat |
| `EVID_CODEX_CHOCOLATE_CXS87` | **Codex Alimentarius** | Codex Standard for Chocolate (CXS 87-1981, Rev. 2016) | Compositional | Min 35% total cocoa solids, min 18% cocoa butter |
| `EVID_FSSAI_CHOCOLATE_2011` | **FSSAI** | Food Products Standards Regulations 2011 (Sec 2.7.4) | Compositional | Min 35% cocoa solids for chocolate sold in India |
